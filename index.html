🎮 Sound & Game States 🎮

How to Play:

WASD to move your character

🍎 GOOD FOOD (Red/Pink) - Increases score (+1)

💀 BAD FOOD (Purple/Black) - Decreases health (-1)

Start health: 5 • Game ends when health reaches 0

Click anywhere to start background music

🔊 Sound effects: Good food (positive chime) • Bad food (negative buzz)
